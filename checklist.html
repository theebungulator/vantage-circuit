The 5-Minute Business Workflow Check

Most small businesses lose hours a week to little things nobody's gotten around to fixing. This takes 5 minutes and tells you exactly where yours are. No sign-up, no catch — just check the boxes that are true for you.

1. Your files

  • My Downloads or Desktop folder has 50+ loose files in it
  • I've lost or hunted for a file I know I saved somewhere
  • I rename/sort files by hand when they pile up

2. Your reports & records

  • I build a report (sales, inventory, hours) by copy-pasting into a document every week or month
  • Formatting that report takes me more than 10 minutes each time
  • I've sent a report late because making it took longer than expected

3. Your website / online presence

  • I don't know the last time I checked if my site was actually up
  • I've found out my site was down from a customer, not myself
  • My site hasn't been updated in 6+ months

4. Repetitive by-hand tasks

  • There's a task I do the exact same way, by hand, at least weekly
  • I've thought "there has to be a faster way to do this" and moved on
  • Nobody else could do this task for me because it's "just how I do it"

What your checks mean

0–2 checked: You're in decent shape — a couple of easy wins are still on the table, but nothing urgent.

3–5 checked: You're losing real time every week to stuff that can be automated in a few days for well under $150.

6+ checked: This is costing you hours you're not getting back. Worth a 10-minute look.
